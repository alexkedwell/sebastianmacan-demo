Liftoff: the phaser flanger built for build-ups and risers

Liftoff is a phaser-flanger designed around one job: making sound take off. Turn the big LAUNCH knob and the whole signal sweeps skyward, climbing higher the further you push it. Hit IGNITE and Liftoff auto-ramps itself into orbit over exactly four seconds, which makes lining it up with the last bar before a drop almost automatic.

A phaser is smooth and watery, a flanger is metallic and jet-like; Liftoff blends both into one launch control, so you can go from gentle underwater motion to full rocket takeoff on a single knob. The WARP control adds Flume-style time-and-space movement inside the sweep for that expensive modern texture, SPEED sets how fast the cycle spins, and STEREO spreads the whole thing wide across the speakers. Every knob is automatable, so you can draw a LAUNCH curve over 8 bars and walk away.

How producers use Liftoff

  • Put Liftoff on the whole mix bus before a drop, stack IGNITE with a riser sample and a drum fill, and the four second auto-ramp lands the takeoff perfectly on the downbeat.
  • Use slow SPEED and low LAUNCH on pads and vocals for constant cinematic motion that never calls attention to itself.
  • Automate LAUNCH on a synth lead through the second half of a phrase to build tension without adding any new elements.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a phaser and a flanger?

Both sweep a moving notch through the sound. A phaser sounds smooth and watery, a flanger sounds metallic and jet-like. Liftoff combines the two under one control so you pick a point on that spectrum instead of choosing a plugin.

Is Liftoff good for EDM build-ups?

That is exactly what it was designed for. The IGNITE auto-ramp is a one-button build-up, and manual LAUNCH rides work for any genre that uses tension and release.

What formats does Liftoff come in?

VST3 and AU for macOS, working in Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper, Studio One and more. Windows is coming soon.
