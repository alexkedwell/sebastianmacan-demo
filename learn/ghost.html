Ghost: the audio freeze plugin that haunts your samples

Ghost is a granular freeze effect for producers who want pads, risers and atmospheres pulled straight out of the audio they already have. Hit FREEZE while your track plays and Ghost traps the last four seconds of sound, holding it in suspended animation while the rest of your session keeps moving. From there you drag around an XY pad to travel through the frozen audio, and every position sounds different: some spots shimmer, some smear, some feel like a tape that has been buried for thirty years.

Because the freeze is granular, a single vocal breath or one held chord becomes an endless, evolving texture instead of a looping stutter. Type in a seed number and you get the exact same haunting back any time, so a happy accident is never lost. Save your favorite spots and recall them instantly.

How producers use Ghost

  • Freeze a vocal, turn the mix fully wet, and record long passes to a new track while riding the XY pad. Chop the best moments and you have a custom pad kit made from your own singer.
  • Freeze the last chord before a drop and let the frozen tail swell underneath the transition. It reads as a riser without sounding like a stock riser sample.
  • Put Ghost on a lo-fi piano loop, blend the frozen layer quietly under the dry sound, and the whole beat gains a haunted, dusty bed that glues everything together.

Frequently asked questions

What does an audio freeze plugin do?

It captures a moment of sound and sustains it indefinitely, like pressing pause on the audio itself. Ghost does this with granular processing, so the frozen moment keeps moving and evolving instead of buzzing on one static loop.

Is Ghost good for ambient and lo-fi music?

Yes. Infinite tails, smeared textures and haunted tape character are the core of the plugin, which makes it a natural fit for ambient, lo-fi hip hop, cinematic beds and experimental sound design.

What formats does Ghost come in?

VST3 and AU for macOS, so it loads in Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper, Studio One and any other modern DAW. Windows builds are in final polish and coming soon.
