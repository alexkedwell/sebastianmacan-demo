Chordsmith: the chord generator plugin that writes in all seven modes

Chordsmith writes chord progressions and matching melodies on demand. Pick one of 20 genres, smash GENERATE, and every click produces a fresh progression with a melody in that style. When something grabs you, drag the MIDI straight out of the plugin onto any track in your DAW and play it with any sound you want. Voicings are genre-true: 7ths and 9ths where they belong, dark minor stacks for Drill, extended church voicings for Gospel.

What separates Chordsmith from other chord generator plugins is the MODE dropdown. Beyond major and minor you get Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian and even Locrian, with every chord in the progression staying true to the mode and its signature color chord featured. Each chord card shows the exact voiced notes inside it, and a per-chord reroll arrow respins just the chord you dislike through new inversions and smart substitutes while the rest of the progression stays put.

How producers use Chordsmith

  • Stuck on a blank project? Pick a genre close to your target, generate until a progression grabs you, reroll the one weak chord, then drag the MIDI out and build the beat around it.
  • Use the mode selector as an education tool: generate the same genre in Dorian, then Lydian, then Phrygian, and hear exactly what each mode does to the mood.
  • Drag out only the melody line and resample it through your own sound design for toplines that started as theory and ended up as texture.

Frequently asked questions

How does a chord generator plugin work?

You pick a genre, key and mode, and Chordsmith assembles a progression from voicings that scene actually uses, plus a melody that fits the chords. You audition inside the plugin and export the MIDI you like.

Which genres does Chordsmith include?

Twenty: Pop, Trap, RnB, Jazz, Gospel, Drill, Afrobeats, Future Bass, House, Techno, Lo-Fi, EDM, Blues, Soul, Reggaeton, K-Pop, Cinematic, Country, Rock and Ambient.

Do I need to know music theory to use it?

No. Generate, listen, keep what you love. If you do know theory, the chord spelling display shows every voiced note so you can see exactly what you are dragging out.
