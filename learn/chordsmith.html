What is a chord generator plugin?

A chord generator plugin (also called a chord plugin, a chord VST or a chord generator VST) writes chord progressions for you. You choose a style and a key, it gives you chords that fit together, and you export them as MIDI. It is a songwriting tool for beatmakers and producers who want to get past a blank project fast, and a way to learn what real progressions look like.

Chordsmith is a MIDI chord generator: what you drag out is MIDI, so the chords play with any instrument you own. It also writes a melody over the chords, so you start with a full musical idea.

How to write a chord progression in three steps

  1. Add Chordsmith as an instrument on a MIDI track.
  2. Pick a genre and press GENERATE. Every click writes a new progression and melody in that style. The arrows next to GENERATE step back and forward through everything you just heard.
  3. Drag the chords or the melody out of Chordsmith onto any track. The MIDI clip names itself with the chords and key, like C#m - C#m7 - G#madd9 (C# Dorian).

20 genres

Pop, Trap, RnB, Jazz, Gospel, Drill, Afrobeats, Future Bass, House, Techno, Lo-Fi, EDM, Blues, Soul, Reggaeton, K-Pop, Cinematic, Country, Rock and Ambient.

Each genre uses voicings the way that scene plays them: 7ths and 9ths where they belong, dark minor stacks for drill, extended church voicings for gospel. So it works as a trap chord generator, a lo-fi chord generator, a gospel chord generator or a house chord generator from the same window.

All seven modes

A MODE dropdown next to KEY gives you Major, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Minor and Locrian. Every chord stays true to the mode you pick, and the mode's signature colour chord always makes it in. Generate the same genre in Dorian, then Lydian, then Phrygian, and you hear exactly what each mode does to the mood.

Every control

  • GENERATE. Writes a complete new progression and melody. It draws on 218 progressions researched from decades of hit songs.
  • NEW CHORDS and NEW MELODY. Reroll just the harmony or just the melody and keep the other half.
  • Per-chord reroll. Respin one chord through new inversions and substitute chords while the rest stays put.
  • COMPLEXITY. Low keeps clean triads and power chords. High brings in 7ths, 9ths, sus2, sus4 and add9.
  • RICHNESS. How tall the chords stack, from tight dyads up to 9ths, 11ths and 13ths.
  • Chord spelling. Every chord card shows the exact notes in the voicing, including inversions.
  • Melody editor. A mini piano roll. Drag notes and they snap to your key and scale.
  • KEY, MODE and METER. Transpose the song, recolour it, or move between 4/4, 3/4 and 6/8.
  • BAR LOOP. Loop any bars while you work on them.
  • SEED. Type the same number and get the same song back.
  • SAVE. Keep a progression and load it in any project.
  • Undo. Fifty steps, inside the plugin.

Ideas to try

  • Stuck on a blank project? Generate until a progression grabs you, reroll the one weak chord, drag the MIDI out and build the beat around it.
  • Drag out only the melody and play it with your own sound for a topline.
  • Turn RICHNESS up while the loop plays to hear the same progression as jazz chords, neo soul chords or simple triads.
  • Flip METER to 6/8 for an instant change of feel without losing the chords.

Frequently asked questions

How does a chord generator plugin work?

You pick a genre, key and mode, and the plugin writes a chord progression that fits together. In Chordsmith you also get a matching melody, and you drag the MIDI onto a track in your DAW.

Do I need to know music theory?

No. Generate, listen, keep what you love. If you do know theory, the chord spelling shows every note in every voicing so you can see exactly what you are dragging out.

Can I export the chords as MIDI?

Yes. Chordsmith is a MIDI chord generator. Drag the chords or the melody out of Chordsmith onto any track. The clip is named with the chords and the key.
