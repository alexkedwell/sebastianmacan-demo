What is granular synthesis?

Granular synthesis chops audio into tiny pieces called grains, then layers, stretches and rearranges those grains into new sound. Play thousands of grains at once and a one second sample becomes a pad that can last as long as you hold the note.

A granular synth plugin is the fastest way to turn a sample into a texture. Producers call the same thing a granular synth VST, or just a granular VST. It is used for ambient music, lo-fi, cinematic sound design, film and game soundscapes, and for pads that do not sound like a preset everybody else has.

How to make a pad from any sample

  1. Put Biome on a MIDI track. It is an instrument, so it goes where your synths go.
  2. Drag any sound onto Biome's window, or click the empty sample box to browse for one.
  3. Play a note. Sound starts when the note starts and stops when it ends. Higher notes lift the texture, lower notes deepen it. Draw a chord and you have a pad.
  4. Turn the knobs while it plays. Every knob morphs the texture.

Play mode and drone mode

PLAY is the default: Biome behaves like a sampler instrument and follows your MIDI notes. Switch to DRONE and Biome plays forever with no notes needed. Record it to a new track while you ride the knobs, then chop the best parts. That is the classic resampling workflow for ambient and drone music.

Time-stretch a sample to your tempo

FIT loops the texture in time with your song over 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars and keeps the sound at the same pitch. FIT+PITCH does the same but lets the pitch change with the speed, the classic sampler repitch sound.

The SPEED knob runs from 4x fast-forward down to zero. At zero it is a freeze effect: time stops and the grain cloud hovers on a single instant. Automate SPEED from 1x to 0 into a drop and the sample freezes mid-air.

The ten knobs

  • POSITION. Where in the sample the grains are picked from.
  • SPRAY. How far grains wander from that position. Low is focused, high blurs the whole sample together.
  • GRAIN SIZE. Small grains sparkle and hiss, big grains stay smooth and recognisable.
  • PITCH. Transposes the texture in semitones.
  • SHIMMER. Some grains jump up an octave and the reverb catches them, like a glowing choir above your sound.
  • MOTION. Slow drift of the grain position over time.
  • SPEED. How fast the grain cloud travels through the sample, from freeze to 4x.
  • TONE. Dark to bright in one knob.
  • SPACE. The built-in reverb.
  • OUTPUT. Final level.

Presets and your own sounds

Biome ships with 25 factory presets and Sebastian's Sounds, 12 presets with the sample already inside, so you can pick one and play. Save your own presets with or without the sample. A/B flips between two versions of your settings.

Two handles on the waveform choose which part of the sample plays. Lock them and Loop Slide moves the whole loop region across the sample from one automation lane.

Ideas to try

  • Sample your own voice, draw a chord, and you have a vocal pad made of you.
  • Drop in a field recording of rain and hold one low note under a lo-fi beat for an ambient bed.
  • Load a single piano or guitar note and turn SHIMMER and SPACE up for a cinematic texture.
  • Use DRONE mode, record two minutes of knob movement, and cut the best four bars as a new sample.

Frequently asked questions

What is a granular synthesizer?

A granular synthesizer chops audio into tiny pieces called grains, then layers and stretches those grains into new sounds. Biome uses this to turn any sample into evolving pads, drones and atmospheres.

How do I make an ambient pad from a sample?

Load the sample into a granular synth, hold a note or a chord, and raise the grain size and reverb. In Biome that is drag a sound in, play a chord, and turn up SPACE.

Can a granular synth time-stretch without changing pitch?

Yes. Biome's FIT locks the texture to 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars at the original pitch. FIT+PITCH is the repitch version, so you get both.

Is Biome a granular VST instrument or a granular VST effect?

Biome is an instrument. It goes on a MIDI track and plays your sample when you play notes, or continuously in DRONE mode.

Which DAWs does Biome work in?

Any DAW that loads VST3 or AU plugins, including Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper and Studio One. macOS gets VST3 and AU, Windows gets VST3.
