Biome: the granular synthesizer that grows textures from any sound

Biome is a granular texture instrument: drag in any audio and it grows the sound into a huge evolving ecosystem of pads, drones and atmospheres. A vocal, a guitar note, a field recording of birds, a crackly vinyl loop, anything works. Granular synthesis chops the audio into tiny grains, then layers and stretches those grains into brand new sound, and Biome wraps that engine in controls that are impossible to get wrong.

In the default PLAY mode Biome behaves like an instrument: sound starts when your MIDI note starts and stops when it ends, higher notes lift the texture and lower notes deepen it, and a drawn chord becomes a pad. FIT loops the texture in time with your song over 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars while keeping the pitch untouched, and FIT+PITCH gives you the classic sampler repitch instead. Factory presets give you starting points, SAVE keeps your own, and A/B lets you flip between two settings to hear which one wins.

How producers use Biome

  • Drop a field recording of rain or birds into Biome, play one low note under your beat, and the texture ducks musically under whatever else is playing.
  • Sample your own vocal, draw a chord in the piano roll, and you have a pad made of you, fitted to tempo with FIT so it loops cleanly.
  • Switch to DRONE mode for the old-school resampling workflow: Biome plays forever with no notes needed, you record it to a new track while riding the knobs, then chop the best parts.

Frequently asked questions

What is a granular synthesizer?

A granular synth slices audio into tiny pieces called grains, then layers, stretches and rearranges those grains into new sound. It is how a one second sample becomes a five minute evolving pad.

What is the difference between PLAY and DRONE mode?

PLAY is MIDI-gated: notes start and stop the sound, and pitch follows the keys. DRONE plays continuously with no MIDI, which suits resampling and ambient workflows.

Can Biome time-stretch without changing pitch?

Yes. FIT is a pure granular time-stretch that locks the texture to 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars at the original pitch. FIT+PITCH is the classic repitch version, so you get both.
