What is a wobble effect?

A wobble effect moves a sound in rhythm. An LFO (a slow repeating wave) turns the filter, the volume, the pitch or the pan up and down in time with the song, so a static sound starts to bounce and breathe. Filter wobble on a bass is the wobble bass sound of dubstep and bass music. Volume wobble is tremolo. Pan wobble is auto-pan. Pitch wobble is vibrato.

Jelly does all four at once from one control. It works as an LFO plugin, a tremolo plugin and an auto-pan in one, which makes it a quick modulation effect for bass, pads, keys, guitars and vocals.

How to make wobble bass in three steps

  1. Put Jelly on your bass track.
  2. Grab the blob. The more it wobbles, the more your sound wobbles.
  3. Pick a RATE. 1/8 is the classic wobble, 1/4 is a slow groove, faster rates get silly in the best way.

Every control

  • WOBBLE. The master amount. How much of everything moves.
  • BOUNCE. The blob behaves like a dropped ball of slime and the sound follows every bounce.
  • SQUISH. Squashes the wobble shape from smooth waves into sharp squelches.
  • MIX. Blends the wobble with your dry sound.
  • RATE. Tempo-synced speed of the wobble.
  • Presets. Six to start from: Init, Chuckle, Jiggle, Slump, Stepper and Wiggle. Save as many of your own as you want.
