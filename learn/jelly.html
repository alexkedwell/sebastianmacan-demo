Jelly: the free wobble plugin that makes everything jiggle

Jelly is a free modulation effect built around one huge wobbling blob. The blob is the control: poke it, and filter, tremolo, pitch jiggle and pan wiggle all move together from a single tempo-synced LFO. The more the blob wobbles, the more your sound wobbles, and because everything shares one clock it always lands on the beat.

WOBBLE is the master amount, deciding how much of everything moves. BOUNCE makes the blob behave like a dropped ball of slime, with the sound following every bounce. SQUISH squashes the wobble shape from smooth waves into sharp squelches, and MIX blends the jiggle with the dry signal. RATE choices run from a slow 1/4 groove through the classic 1/8 wobble into rates that get silly in the best way.

How producers use Jelly

  • Automate WOBBLE from zero to full across a 4-bar transition. The track melts into jelly right before the drop, then snaps back clean on the downbeat.
  • Put Jelly on a bass with SQUISH turned up for instant wobble bass movement in dubstep, house and bass music.
  • Add 30 percent MIX on a static pad or loop and it quietly starts breathing in time without sounding processed.

Frequently asked questions

What is a wobble effect?

It moves the sound in rhythm: volume, filter, pitch and pan wiggling together so static sounds bounce and breathe. It is the backbone of wobble bass, but it works on pads, keys, guitars and even vocals.

Is Jelly really free?

Yes, free forever. Enter your email and the download starts. Full version, no locked knobs, no trial timer.

Can Jelly replace an LFO tool for basic duties?

For musical, tempo-synced wobble on filter, volume, pitch and pan, yes, and it is far faster to dial in. It is a creative effect rather than a modular routing system.

Which DAWs does Jelly work in?

Any macOS DAW that loads VST3 or AU plugins, including Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper and Studio One. Windows is coming soon.
