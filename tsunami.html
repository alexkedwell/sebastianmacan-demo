What Tsunami does

Press one key and Tsunami plays the whole chord: five notes, each stacked with seven detuned saws, spread across the stereo field, with a clean sub underneath. That is 35 oscillators of future bass per finger. Pick the chord flavor from a menu and everything you play is instantly in that color. No voicing knowledge required, no six-finger stretches.

Chord flavors

MAJOR, MINOR, MAJOR 7, MINOR 7, SUS 4, ADD 9, MINOR 9, POWER OCT. Minor 9 is the future bass classic. Power Oct is for when the drop needs to hit like a truck.

The knobs

WIDTH. Unison detune plus stereo spread. Zero is a polite mono saw. Full is the wall of sound the genre is named after.
CUTOFF. The easy filter knob. One big lowpass over everything. Automate it into the drop like it is 2016.
WOBBLE. An LFO grabs the filter and breathes it. More knob = deeper and faster. The sun on the panel pulses with it.
PUMP. Fake sidechain. Every note you play ducks the volume and lets it swell back, so the chords bounce like they are fighting a kick drum, even without one.
SUB. A clean sine one octave below the root, dead center. The saws are the shine, the sub is the power.
ATTACK / RELEASE. Shape the swell. Slow attack plus long release is the classic future bass "chord bloom".
OUT. Output trim, gently saturated so slamming it makes it thicker, not uglier.

Ride the wave

The ocean on the panel is the sound. Play a chord and the sea swells, the crest curls and throws spray, PUMP visibly dips the tide, and WOBBLE makes the sunset breathe. When the wave is huge, so is the mix.
