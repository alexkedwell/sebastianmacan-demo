What Chop does

Drop it on a pad, a vocal, a guitar loop, a whole bus. Chop opens and closes on the beat so the sound turns into a rhythm. Six rates from quarter notes to 1/32s with triplets, five patterns from straight to gallop to random, and a LENGTH knob that decides how much of every step you hear. SHAPE goes from a soft pluck to a hard gate. SWING and HUMANIZE keep it from sounding like a machine.

Same family as Smear, Stutter and Twitch

Chop shares its panel, its chomper and its engine with the three $15 boxes. Each one does one thing; this one happens to be free. Nothing held back.

Six knobs, one groove

RATE. 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, 1/32. Locked to your DAW tempo.
PATTERN. STRAIGHT, OFFBEAT, 3-3-2, GALLOP, RANDOM. Which steps open.
LENGTH. How much of each step stays open. Short for stabs, long for a pulse.
SHAPE. Soft pluck at zero, hard gate at full. 2 ms edges so it never clicks.
SWING. Every second step lands late, up to 66% MPC swing.
HUMANIZE. Up to 12 ms of timing drift and up to 6 dB of level drift, random per step.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Chop on something that sustains. A pad, a held chord, a vocal, a reverb return.
2. Press play. It opens on Init at 1/8 straight. You already have a groove.
3. Pick a pattern and a rate. GALLOP at 1/16 is the trap move. 3-3-2 at 1/8 is the latin one. RANDOM is the surprise.

Make it yours

THE CHOMPER. The green face is the readout. It bites every time the gate opens, so you see the pattern before you hear it.
MIX and OUT. Blend the chopped signal with the dry one, trim the result. BYPASS pill in the header for an honest A/B.
PRESETS. 11 to start: Init, Nibble, Machine Gun, Gentle Hiccup, Gallop, Chomp, Triplet Bite, Swing Snack, Three Three Two, Offbeat Munch and Feral. Save unlimited of your own.
INIT ANY KNOB. Right-click or Cmd-click a knob to send it home.
Window size. Drag the corner grip to resize the whole plugin; your DAW remembers it.
