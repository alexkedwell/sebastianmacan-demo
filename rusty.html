What Rusty does

Rusty is five robots living in one box. Drop it on a vocal, a synth, anything with a voice, and it comes out the other side as a machine. The robot on the front panel changes with the mode, glows with the sound, and does exactly what it looks like it does.

Meet the robots

CHROME. The classic. A 16-band vocoder that follows your pitch and shines like a disco android. Vocoder chords on a synth are instant robot funk.
TALKBOX. The melodic one. Hard formants and pitch snapped to the nearest note. The robot that sings hooks.
DALEK. The angry intercom. Ring modulation and grit. Say something normal, hear something menacing.
TOYBOT. The 80s toy. Crushed, narrow and adorable, like a battery-powered friend giving instructions through a tiny speaker.
SUB BOT. The bass monster. Built for wubs, dubstep and space house: it tracks your pitch and generates a huge talking sub layer with a tempo-synced wub filter. Vocals become talking bass lines. Watch his subwoofer belly pulse.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Rusty on a vocal or synth. Anything with pitch and personality.
2. Pick your robot. The character on the panel switches with you.
3. Turn TWIST. One big knob morphs how deep into the machine you go.

Make it yours

TWIST. The hero knob. Each robot changes character as you turn it.
VOICE. Dry/wet. Sneak the robot under your real voice or go full machine.
PITCH. Shift the robot up or down by semitones. Deep bots and chipmunk bots welcome.
GROWL. Drive and aggression. Every robot has a temper.
AIR. Adds your natural breath and sibilance back on top so words stay clear.
Presets, A/B and bypass. 14 factory robots to start from, save your own, compare two setups, and a true bypass button.

FAQ

How do I make my vocals sound like a robot?

Put Rusty on your vocal track, pick a robot, turn TWIST. That is the whole tutorial.

Is Rusty really free?

Yes. Full version, all five robots, no locked knobs, no trial timer.

Does it work on synths and bass too?

Absolutely. Chrome on synth chords is instant robot funk, and Sub Bot was built for basses and wubs in the first place.

Windows builds are finished and getting their final polish. macOS is live today and Windows lands soon. Join the email list when you download and you will hear the moment it drops.
