What Gloss does

Gloss makes tracks sit right in the mix with four knobs and zero engineering degree. Tell it what the track is, then shape it with CLEAN, PUNCH, GLOW and WIDTH while auto-gain keeps the volume honest.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Gloss on a track. Vocal, drums, bass, or the whole master: Gloss works anywhere.
2. Tell it what it is hearing. Pick vocal, drums, bass or master. Gloss tunes everything under the hood for that job.
3. Turn the four knobs. CLEAN, PUNCH, GLOW, WIDTH. Stop when it sounds good. That is the whole manual.

Make it yours

CLEAN. Clears mud and boxiness so the track stops fighting the rest of the mix.
PUNCH. Brings out the hit and snap. Drums wake up, vocals get forward.
GLOW. The expensive top end. Air on vocals, sheen on the master.
WIDTH. Opens the stereo image. Auto-gain keeps louder from fooling your ears the whole time.

Pro tip

Because auto-gain matches the volume, whatever sounds better with Gloss actually IS better. Trust your first instinct and move on.
