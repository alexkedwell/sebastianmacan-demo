What Stutter does

Use it on drum loops, especially drum and bass. It also gets weird variations out of your vocals, your synths, any instrument really.

Stutter grabs a slice of whatever is playing and fires it again. Not on every beat: AMOUNT is the dice, the chance that a repeat happens on each step of the grid. RATE picks the grid from whole bars down to 1/32. LENGTH says how long the stutter lasts and REPEATS says how many times it fires. ROLL speeds the repeats up or slows them down. PITCH moves every repeat up to an octave either way. REVERSE, TAPE, SNIP, FADE, FILTER and CRUSH turn one repeat into a trick. HOLD freezes the buffer while you lean on it.

Jitter and the patch bay

The purple creature is Jitter. It twitches with every repeat. Next to it is the patch bay: HOLD plugs the dangling cable into the free socket, and you can click any cable to grey it out. The cables are toys. Nothing breaks if you play with them.

Sixteen controls, one grid

AMOUNT. The chance a repeat fires on each step. 0% is silence from the engine, 100% is every step.
RATE. Slice size, 1/1 down to 1/32. Pick FREE and it ignores the tempo: 125 ms steps, transport stopped or not.
LENGTH. How long one stutter lasts. AUTO is the classic quick trip. Set it anywhere from 1/8 to 2 BARS and the repeats keep coming until time is up.
REPEATS. How many times the slice fires. More repeats, smaller slices.
ROLL. The repeats speed up or slow down across the stutter. Right is FASTER. Left is SLOWER. Hello, drum roll.
REVERSE. Chance a burst plays backwards. Instant tape-rewind moments.
PITCH and GLIDE. True pitch, -12 to +12 semitones, noon is 0. GLIDE ramps the pitch across the burst.
TAPE. Chance a burst becomes a tape-stop or tape-start.
SNIP. Cuts every repeat short and leaves a gap. A little is a trim. A lot is a row of tiny blips.
FADE. The whole stutter fades out or swells in. Left is OUT. Right is IN.
FILTER. Sweeps a low-pass closed or a high-pass open across the burst.
CRUSH. Gritty lo-fi repeats. Only the repeats get chewed. Your dry signal stays clean.
DECLICK. Finds the hard edges a repeat copies in and smooths only those few milliseconds. The other side of the knob is PUNCH.
PATTERN. DICE is the classic. EVERY BAR, EVERY 2 BARS, FILL 4, ACCEL and TRIPLET lock the bursts to the song.
SEED. Same seed, same dice, every time. Change it for a new roll.
HOLD. Freezes the buffer and fires it for as long as you hold.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Stutter on a loop. Drums are the classic. It opens on Init at 1/8, so you hear it working immediately.
2. Set AMOUNT. A quarter turn is a fill every few bars. Full is a machine gun.
3. Add one trick. REVERSE for rewinds, PITCH up an octave for the chipmunk, TAPE for stops, CRUSH for grit. Then save it.

Make it yours

MIX and OUT. Blend the repeats with the dry loop, trim the result. BYPASS pill in the header for an honest A/B.
ON and REPEAT pills. ON dims the box and passes dry. REPEAT is the classic engine; switch it off and TAPE, REVERSE, PITCH and FILTER act on the live signal per step instead.
INIT ANY KNOB. Right-click or Cmd-click a knob to send it home.
Window size. Opens compact. Drag the corner grip to make it as big as you like; your DAW remembers it.

Common questions

Is Stutter really free? Yes. Enter your email and the download starts right away. Full version, no locked knobs, no trial timer.
Does Stutter work without the transport running? Yes. With the transport stopped it runs on its own clock. Pick FREE in RATE and it ignores the tempo altogether. Jam on it live.
I have an older Stutter. What changes? Five knobs were swapped for LENGTH, ROLL, FADE, SNIP and CRUSH. Projects and presets that used the old five will not sound the same, so keep your old installer if a song depends on it.
What DAWs does Stutter work in? Any DAW that loads VST3 or AU plugins: Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper, Studio One and more.
Is there a Windows version of Stutter? Yes. Stutter ships for macOS (VST3 + AU) and Windows (VST3), code-signed on both. One download covers both platforms.
How do I install Stutter? Run the installer and it puts the plugin files in the right folders. Restart your DAW, rescan if needed, done.
