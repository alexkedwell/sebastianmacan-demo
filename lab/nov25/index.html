Beat Machine

LATE BIRD

Late on purpose.

▶ WITH SOUND

The video shows the groove grid only: a dead straight 60 BPM beat, then the same beat with the hits moved.

What it does

A drum machine with a groove grid. Load a kick, a snare and a hat, or a whole sample pack. Sixteen steps per lane. Drag any hit late or early and the beat stops sounding like a computer.

Controls

SWINGpushes every second sixteenth late, up to full triplet swing
AMOUNT, per lanehow much of the groove the kick, the snare and the hats each take
Drag a dotnudge one hit late or early, in milliseconds
PresetsSLEEPY HEAD and TIP TOE

Four looks, same controls

1 Night sky branch. 2 Sunrise bedroom. 3 Dark studio neon. 4 Toy store candy.

Where it goes next

  • Drum machine first: load your own one-shots, program the 16 steps, shape the groove.
  • Pack mode: point it at a sample pack folder. It sorts the pack into kicks, snares, hats, chords, pads and loops, reads the key and tempo of the melodic files, and builds a beat from it.
  • SWAP on any lane brings up another sound from the pack in the same key. GENERATE picks a whole new set, in a new key if you want. SWAP ALL replaces everything.
  • One machine, two pages (BEAT and GROOVE), built in two stages. Stage one is the drum machine. Stage two adds pack mode.

Honest limits. Prototype works on separate drum sounds. Moving a hit early needs a little look-ahead in a real plugin. Pack mode is designed, not prototyped.

Ghost Note Maker

BOO TAPS

Tiny hits between the hits.

▶ WITH SOUND

A stiff 80 BPM beat, then ghosts on, more ghosts, quiet to loud, loose timing, the dice, both presets, and off and on at the end.

What it does

Put it on a snare or a whole drum loop. It listens for the hits and adds quiet ghost notes in the gaps, the little taps a real drummer plays without thinking.

Controls

AMOUNThow many ghost hits
LEVELhow quiet they are, from 20 dB to 3 dB under the real hit
FEELhow loose the timing is, up to 22 ms late or 13 ms early
DICEthree ghost patterns
PresetsSOFT HANDS and BUSY FINGERS

Where it goes next

  • Alex's verdict on Nov 25: awesome. Next step is a real plugin prototype.
  • Needs host tempo, live hit detection and its own level matching.

Honest limits. In the prototype a ghost is a quieter, shorter, darker copy of the hit before it. Tested on one clean programmed beat.

Drum Tuner

TUNA

Drums in key. One knob.

▶ WITH SOUND

Melody in F minor. The kick starts out of tune on purpose, then TUNA moves it to F and the snare to C. Best on headphones: the kick sits around 44 Hz.

What it does

Pick the key of the song. TUNA hears the pitch of the kick and the snare and slides each one to a note that fits.

Controls

KEYthe note the song is in
TUNE0 to 100 percent, how far each drum slides to the right note
KICK FINE and SNARE FINEnudge one drum by ear, in cents
SNAP TONEAREST, ROOT or FIFTH
DisplayHEARD F#1, PLAYS F1, per drum, with an in tune meter
PresetsROOT LOCK and HIGH FIVE

Where it goes next

  • A real plugin needs a short LEARN step, because a low kick takes time to read.
  • One instance per drum, with a shifter that keeps the attack.

Honest limits. Moving a drum a long way changes its length and tone, not only its pitch. The snare in the video goes up three semitones and gets shorter and brighter.

Tail Shaper

GECKO

Short and tight. Or long and washy.

▶ WITH SOUND

Normal hits, short and tight, a sweep from short to long, then HOLD, SMOOTH and both presets. In the prototype the kick tail goes from 257 ms down to 71 ms, or up to 575 ms.

What it does

One knob for the tail of every drum hit. Left makes hits short and tight. Right lets them ring out. The front of the hit is never touched.

Controls

LENGTHleft is shorter, middle is no change, right is longer
HOLDhow much of the body is kept before shaping starts
SMOOTHa softer fade on the short side, a darker tail on the long side
Displaythe real waveform, grey for the original and colour for the shaped hit
PresetsSNAP PEA and BIG SPLASH

Where it goes next

  • Make the long side stretch the drum's own decay.
  • Add level matching so short does not also mean quieter.

Honest limits. The long side is an added splash behind the hit in this prototype. The short side is also about 3 dB quieter.

Kick and Bass Glue

SIDEKICK

The bass makes room for the kick.

▶ WITH SOUND

Off, then on, then each knob, then both presets, then off and on at the end.

What it does

Put it on the bass. It hears the kick and dips the low end of the bass under every kick, then lets it swell back. No sidechain routing.

Controls

GLUEhow deep the bass dips
TIMEhow fast the bass swells back, 40 to 700 ms
FOCUShow much of the bass is affected, from only the deep sub to more of the bass
PresetsGENTLE HUG and BIG PUMP

Where it goes next

  • Test on real 808 glides and a played bass.
  • A real plugin needs a sensitivity knob for the kick detector and a little look-ahead.

Honest limits. Tested on one beat with a synthesized bass.

Hat Humanizer

LOOSE GOOSE

Hats played by hand.

▶ WITH SOUND

Machine hats, human on, then each knob alone, all together, both presets, and off and on at the end.

What it does

Programmed hats all sound the same. LOOSE GOOSE makes every hat a little different in level, brightness and timing, so they sound played.

Controls

HUMANthe big knob; turns LEVEL, TONE and TIMING up together
LEVELup to 6 dB louder or softer per hat
TONEeach hat a little darker or brighter
TIMINGup to 14 ms early or late
ACCENTbeats louder, in-between hats softer
PresetsGENTLE HANDS and SLEEPY STICKS

Where it goes next

  • Prove it is click-free on busy hat loops.
  • Needs host tempo and seeded random so a bounce sounds the same twice.

Honest limits. Not yet proven click-free with the effect on. Tested on one clean programmed beat.
