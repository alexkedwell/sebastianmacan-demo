What Chordsmith does

Chordsmith writes chord progressions and melodies for you. Pick a genre, smash GENERATE, and you get a fresh progression with a matching melody every single click. When you hear one you love, drag the MIDI straight into your DAW and it is yours.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Chordsmith on a track. In your DAW, add Chordsmith as an instrument on a MIDI track.
2. Pick a genre and hit GENERATE. Every click writes a brand new progression and melody in that style. Flipping too fast? The little < and > arrows next to GENERATE walk you back and forward through everything you just heard, so a great one is never lost.
3. Drag the MIDI into your DAW. Grab the chords or the melody right out of Chordsmith and drop them onto any track. The clip even names itself with the actual chords and key, like C#m - C#m7 - G#madd9 (C# Dorian), so your session stays readable. Now play them with any sound you want.

See inside every chord

Chord spelling. Every chord card shows the exact notes inside it, in the order they are voiced. A C chord might be C E G, or E G C if it is inverted. You see the voicing before you drag it out, the way advanced players want it.
Write in any mode. A MODE dropdown next to KEY unlocks all seven modes: Major, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Minor and even Locrian. Dorian gives you that French-house glow, Lydian floats like melodic house at sunrise, Phrygian goes full dark techno. Every chord in the progression stays true to the mode, with its signature color chord featured. The whole candy case of flavors, in one dropdown.
Per-chord reroll. Love three chords but not the fourth? Hit the little reroll arrow on just that card. Chordsmith spins that one chord through new inversions and smart substitute chords that fit the slot, while the rest of your progression stays put. Keep clicking until the whole thing sounds like yours.
Save the magic ones. Found a progression you love? Hit SAVE in the PROGRESSIONS bar and name it. It comes back exactly as you left it, rerolled chords and all, in every session, forever.
Change key without losing the song. Flip KEY and your song transposes. Flip MODE and the same song gets recolored. Flip METER for 4/4, waltz, shuffle and more. Only GENERATE writes a new song, everything else shapes the one you have.

Every control, explained

GENERATE. The big one. Every press writes a complete new progression and matching melody in your genre, key, mode and meter. Powered by the HITMAKER engine: 218 progressions researched from decades of actual hit songs, so every roll starts from harmony that is already proven to work.
NEW CHORDS and NEW MELODY dice. Two smaller dice next to GENERATE. NEW CHORDS rerolls just the harmony and never touches a note of your melody. NEW MELODY writes a fresh melody over the chords you kept. Mix and match until both halves are yours.
COMPLEXITY. How colorful the chords get. Low keeps it to clean triads and power chords. High invites 7ths, 9ths, sus2, sus4 and add9 colors in, weighted by genre, so Jazz at full tilt gets far spicier than Rock at full tilt.
RICHNESS. How tall the chords stack. Left leans to tight dyads and triads, center is the classic Chordsmith voicing, right reaches for 9ths, 11ths and 13ths. Drag it live while the loop plays: the same progression re-voices under your ears without rerolling anything. Double-click snaps back to center.
MELODY editing. The melody strip is a real mini piano roll with its own keys down the side. Grab any note and drag it: it snaps to your current key and scale, and the clip plays the change instantly. If a melody note rubs against a new chord behind it, the note glows amber so you can hear it, see it, and decide - your notes are never auto-corrected behind your back.
KEY, MODE and METER. KEY transposes your song. MODE recolors it through all seven modes. METER moves between 4/4, 3/4 waltz and 6/8 shuffle, re-phrasing the melody so it feels like the meter while the chords stay put. Only GENERATE writes a new song.
BACK and FORWARD history. Everything you do - every generate, reroll, dice roll and loaded progression - is a step you can walk back through, fifty steps deep. Step back onto a saved progression and it comes back name and all.
SEED. The number behind the song. Type the same seed and you get the same song back, every time. Trade seeds with a friend like cheat codes.
PROGRESSIONS bar. SAVE names and keeps your current song forever, rerolls, melody edits and all. Load any saved progression from the dropdown in any session, in any project.
