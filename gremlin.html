What Gremlin does

Gremlin grabs your audio, slices it on the beat grid, and breaks it on purpose. Stutters, rewinds, octave jumps, backwards chunks, all tempo-synced so the wreckage still grooves. It was born as the Glitch mode inside Rusty, our robot voice box, and it kept chewing through the wiring until it earned its own cage. Drums are its favorite food, but it will happily demolish vocals, synths and whole mixes.

The controls

BREAK. The hero knob. How broken. At 10 percent it is a tasteful stutter at the end of a bar. At 100 it is a machine eating itself in rhythm.
SLICE. Tempo-synced slice length: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, or a free length when you want it off the grid.
REPEAT. How many times a slice stutters, 1 to 16. Low is a hiccup, high is a machine gun.
REVERSE. The chance any slice plays backwards. Instant rewind moments without touching an edit.
PITCH. Octave-jump steps on repeats. Stutters that climb up or crash down while they fire.
CHAOS. Random re-slicing. The gremlin stops following the plan and starts improvising.
SEED. The leash. Every random decision comes from this number, so the same seed replays the same glitches, take after take, bounce after bounce.
MIX. Dry/wet. Sprinkle glitches over the clean beat or go full wreck.
BYPASS. One click back to civilization.

Auto pilot or smash the button

Two ways to drive. Auto mode lets Gremlin decide when to strike, using BREAK as its appetite. Manual mode hands you the GLITCH! pad: a momentary button that glitches only while you hold it. Punch in a fill on the last beat of the bar, let go, and the loop walks out clean like nothing happened.

Twelve flavors of broken

Factory presets with names that tell you exactly what they do: Machine Gun, Rewind, Broken Toy, Stairs Down and eight more. Twelve starting points from polite fill generator to full meltdown.

What to feed it

Drum loops are the classic: instant fills, turnarounds and edits you would never program by hand. Vocals become stammering hooks. Pads and chords get shredded into rhythmic confetti. Put it on the master for two bars before the drop if you are feeling brave.
