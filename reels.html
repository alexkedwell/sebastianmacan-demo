What Reels does

Reels is a tape machine with the reels actually spinning. Three genuinely different machines, warm tape squash, living wow and flutter, real crackle and hum that breathe under your playing, and an AGE knob that adds years of wear. It never does too much: your Rhodes still sounds like your Rhodes, just like it got printed to beautiful old tape.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Reels on a track. Drums love it, bass loves it, whole mixes love it. Start on the master bus for instant glue.
2. Pick a machine. STUDIO is big warm reel-to-reel, CASSETTE is a bedroom four-track, VINYL brings the crackle. Each one is a different decade.
3. Open the presets. 14 machines already dialed in, starting with Ampex Dream for keys. Or push the input until you smile and save your own.

Make it yours

HEAD BUMP. The low-end lift real tape machines add. This is where drums get that vintage weight.
WOW and FLUTTER. The gentle pitch drift of real tape. A little makes things feel human, a lot sounds like a warped memory.
AGE. Years of wear in one knob. Dulls the top, softens the edges, adds character.
HISS. Real tape noise, dosed to taste. It ducks under your playing and breathes back in the gaps, like real hardware.
CRACKLE and HUM. Vinyl dust clicks and a whisper of electrical hum. The secret sauce on lo-fi beds.
PRESETS + A/B. 14 factory machines, save unlimited of your own, and flip A/B to compare two setups instantly.

Pro tip

Load the Ampex Dream preset on Rhodes or keys lofi chords. That is the sound Reels was built around. Then print your drum bus through CASSETTE with medium AGE and blend it under the clean bus: instant lo-fi weight without losing the punch.

Common questions

What does tape emulation add to a track? Tape softens harsh digital edges, adds warm saturation, and drifts slightly in pitch (wow and flutter), which is a big part of the lo-fi and vintage sound. Reels models a real tape machine so your drums, keys and full mixes get that glue without the hardware.
How is Reels different from other tape plugins? Most tape plugins pile on effects until your track sounds like a different track. Reels is tuned for subtlety: the machines, noise and wobble are dosed so your sound keeps its character and just gains the warmth. If you want your Rhodes to still be your Rhodes, this is the tape for it.
