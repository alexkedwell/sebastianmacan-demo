What Fireplace does

Fireplace puts a room around your music. Drop it on your master or a bus and it lays living atmosphere UNDER the beat: a crackling fire, rain on a window, a dusty attic record player, an old tape machine room. It is not a noise loop. The fire, the attic and the tape room are generated live and never repeat. The rain is a real forest downpour Sebastian recorded himself, played back in random windows with slow crossfades so it never loops either. And the whole thing listens to your music so it can get out of the way.

Pick your room

FIREPLACE. The classic. Warm low roar, snapping crackle bursts, embers fizzing up the chimney. The fire in the window actually burns, and it burns brighter as you turn it up.
RAIN ON WINDOW. A real forest rainstorm. Steady, soft and endless. Leave it running all night while you work. Rain on the glass, you inside, warm.
VINYL ATTIC. A dusty record spinning in the corner: needle hiss, dust ticks, turntable rumble. Instant "found this beat in a crate" energy.
TAPE ROOM. The machine room: tape hiss with a slow wow drift, faint mains hum, blinking meters. For beats that want to sound like they were left running overnight.

The knobs

AMOUNT. How much room you pour under the music. The fire grows with it.
DUCK. The secret weapon. The ambience listens to your music and tucks itself under every hit, then swells back in the gaps. Your beat breathes.
TONE. Dark and woolly to bright and crisp. Same room, different mood.
DISTANCE. Pull the room close or push it to the far wall. Closes the top end and sinks the level like real distance does.
OUT. Output trim, plus a true bypass button.

Why it beats a noise sample

A crackle sample loops. You hear the loop, your listeners hear the loop, and it sits ON TOP of your mix fighting the drums. Fireplace never repeats: three rooms are generated live from scratch, and the rain room plays a long real recording in shuffled windows. The DUCK knob keeps the atmosphere underneath the music where it belongs. That is the difference between an ambience layer and a WAV file.
