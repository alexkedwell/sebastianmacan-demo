What Twitch does

Twitch is a rack of twelve effects that all fire on the same tempo grid. Pick a RATE, then turn up what you want: GATE for trance gates, CRUSH for bit and sample-rate decimation, WARP for tape-stop dives and tape-start rises on every step, COMB for a tuned ring, GRAIN for a rhythm-locked grain cloud, GLITCH for buffer jumps and micro-repeats, FORMANT for a vowel sweep, RING for a modulator that swoops down to its pitch each step. CHAOS re-rolls every knob you turned up, once a bar, around where you left it. And SKETCH is the draw pad.

Zappy and the sketch pad

The cyan creature is Zappy. It twitches with whatever you touch. Under it is the pad: draw 16 bars across one bar of music. TIME mode reads the audio back in time by what you drew, the Gross Beat move. VOL mode is a drawn gate. MAD mode turns each bar you drew into a random seeded event, micro-repeat, reverse, crush or a ring hit, as hard as you drew it. Same drawing, same SEED, same result.

Twelve knobs, one grid

RATE and FREE. The step grid every knob fires on, 1/1 down to 1/32, or FREE in milliseconds with the transport stopped.
GATE. Trance gate on the grid. SHAPE of the gate follows the step.
CRUSH. Bit-crush and decimate, with drive. Full is about 4 bits.
WARP. Per-step pitch bend. Left is a tape-stop dive, right is a tape-start rise.
COMB and TUNE. A tuned feedback comb that rings on each event, 40 Hz to 2 kHz.
GRAIN. Freeze-and-scrub grain cloud of the captured step.
GLITCH. Chance per step of a buffer jump, a skip or a micro-repeat.
FORMANT. Vowel morph, A to U, swept per step.
RING and HZ. Ring modulator, 20 Hz to 2 kHz, swooping down to its pitch on every step.
CHAOS. Once a bar, re-rolls every other knob around where you left it. A knob at zero stays at zero, so turn something up first.
SKETCH and MODE. How much of the drawing you hear, and whether it is TIME, VOL or MAD.
SEED. Same seed, same chaos, same MAD events. Change it for a new roll.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Twitch on a loop. Drums or a chord loop. It opens on Init with everything at zero, so it passes clean.
2. Turn up one knob. GATE at half is a trance gate. CRUSH at half is a broken radio. WARP left is a tape stop on every step.
3. Draw on the pad. Drag across it, set MODE to TIME, turn SKETCH up. Right-click clears it.

Make it yours

MIX and OUT. Blend the rack with the dry signal, trim the result. BYPASS pill in the header for an honest A/B.
ON pill. The switch in the panel corner. Off dims the box and passes dry.
INIT ANY KNOB. Right-click or Cmd-click a knob to send it home.
Window size. Opens compact. Drag the corner grip to make it as big as you like; your DAW remembers it.
