What Twitch does

Put it on a drum loop. Twitch and Stutter are brothers, and together they make drum and bass.

Twitch is a rack of twelve effects that all fire on the same tempo grid. Pick a RATE, then turn up what you want. HALF drops the loop to half speed. REPEAT throws in stutter fills. REVERSE plays pieces backwards. STOP is a tape stop. SCRATCH rocks the audio back and forth like a record. GATE chops a trance pattern, PUMP ducks on every beat, and FILTER, DRIVE, LIQUID and PAN shape each step. SKETCH is the draw pad.

Zappy and the sketch pad

The cyan creature is Zappy. It twitches with whatever you touch. Hit the bolt button next to ON and a storm rolls in behind it, striking harder when the rack fires. Under it is the pad: draw 16 bars across one bar of music. TIME mode reads the audio back in time by what you drew. VOL mode turns each slot down by what you drew. FILTER mode closes the filter on each slot by what you drew. Same drawing, same result, every bar.

Twelve effects, one grid

RATE and FREE. The step grid every knob fires on, 1/1 down to 1/32, or FREE in milliseconds.
HALF. Half-speed playback, blended in as you turn it up.
REPEAT. Stutter fills. A slice of the step repeats, more often the higher you go.
REVERSE. Plays what you just heard backwards.
STOP. Tape stop. The pitch dives to nothing, then the beat comes back.
SCRATCH. Vinyl scratch. The audio rocks back and forth.
GATE. Trance gate pattern on the grid.
PUMP. Ducks on every beat, like a sidechain.
FILTER. A low-pass that closes over each step.
DRIVE. Saturation that hits at the top of each step.
LIQUID. A flanger sweep.
PAN. Auto-pan, side to side on the grid.
SKETCH and MODE. How much of the drawing you hear, and whether it is TIME, VOL or FILTER.
SEED. Picks the pattern for REPEAT, REVERSE, STOP, SCRATCH and GATE. Same seed, same fills. Change it for a new roll.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Twitch on a loop. Drums or a chord loop. It opens on Init with everything at zero, so it passes clean.
2. Turn up one knob. GATE at half is a trance gate. HALF all the way up is half time. STOP drops a tape stop in now and then.
3. Draw on the pad. Drag across it, set MODE to TIME, turn SKETCH up. Right-click clears it.

Make it yours

MIX and OUT. Blend the rack with the dry signal, trim the result. BYPASS pill in the header for an honest A/B.
ON pill. The switch in the panel corner. Off dims the box and passes dry.
INIT ANY KNOB. Right-click or Cmd-click a knob to send it home.
Window size. Opens compact. Drag the corner grip to make it as big as you like; your DAW remembers it.

Common questions

Is Twitch really free? Yes. Enter your email and the download starts right away. Full version, no locked knobs, no trial timer.
Does every Twitch knob run at once? Yes. Each knob is its own effect on the same grid. Turn up one, or all of them.
I have Twitch 1. What changes? Version 2 is a new engine with new knobs, and it installs over version 1. Songs and presets made with the old knobs will not sound the same, so keep version 1 if a project depends on it.
What DAWs does Twitch work in? Any DAW that loads VST3 or AU plugins: Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, GarageBand, Reaper, Studio One and more.
Is there a Windows version of Twitch? Yes. Twitch ships for macOS (VST3 + AU) and Windows (VST3), code-signed on both. One download covers both platforms.
How do I install Twitch? Run the installer and it puts the plugin files in the right folders. Restart your DAW, rescan if needed, done.
