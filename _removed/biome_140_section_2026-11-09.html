It's always the broken ones, right?

Biome 1.4.0 came out on August 31, 2026. It has crackles and pops. It has three knobs (TEXTURE, MIX, OUT) that are all basically volume knobs doing the same job. And it has a tone I never managed to get back. Unpolished, a little rough at the edges, and sometimes that roughness is exactly the thing.

1.6.5 is still my favorite. It is cleaner, it has the presets, the sounds, FIT, INIT, all of it. But some of the best textures I have made came out of 1.4.0, so you get both. Every Biome order includes the 1.4.0 installer as a separate download, and they install side by side. Both show up as "Biome" in your DAW; open the preset menu and 1.6.5 says its version at the bottom. Or just look at the window: this is what 1.4.0 looks like.

Biome 1.4.0 · August 31, 2026
How to tell them apart. 1.4.0 has 13 knobs in two rows, with TEXTURE, DUCK, MIX and OUT along the bottom. 1.6.5 has 10 knobs, a sample box that says "DROP A SOUND", and v1.6.5 at the end of the preset menu.
1.4.0 runs on Mac and Windows. Same August code, built for both. It installs beside the current Biome and stays exactly as it was.
