What Pocket does

Snare not sounding right in the car? Pocket moves the snare back into the room. One DEPTH knob does the whole job: it softens the top end the way air does, pushes the snare a few milliseconds back, opens up the room around it and tucks the level. Dead centre the whole way. Your snare stops fighting the vocal and starts living with the drums.

Where it comes from

Orbit, the free auto-pan, had a side effect nobody planned: it mixed hats in. The darkening and the tiny pre-delay it used to fake distance turned out to be the useful part. Pocket is that depth engine with the motion stripped out and the source pinned to the centre, rebuilt for the one drum that needs it most.

One big knob, five helpers

DEPTH. The knob. Together it darkens the top from 16 kHz down to 4.5 kHz, adds 0 to 12 ms of distance pre-delay, brings up the room and dips the level up to 3 dB. Zero is your dry snare. Turn it up and the snare walks to the back of the room without ever leaving the centre.
CRACK. The transient. Pull it back to tuck the hit under the beat, push it to snap. Centre is untouched.
BODY. 200 Hz, plus or minus 6 dB. Thicken a thin snare or clean up a boomy one. Centre is flat.
ROOM. Early reflections plus a short tail, 80 to 450 ms. The first reflection is mono, so the direct sound stays centred no matter how big the room gets.
WIDTH. Stereo width of the room only, never the direct. The snare stays mono-solid while the space around it opens up.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Pocket on the snare. Snare track, snare bus, or the whole top kit. It opens on Init, which is close to dry.
2. Turn DEPTH until the snare sits. Somewhere between 30 and 60 percent it stops poking out and starts sounding placed. That is the pocket.
3. Shape it. CRACK for the hit, BODY for the weight, ROOM and WIDTH for the space. MIX blends it with the dry signal, OUTPUT trims the result.

Make it yours

DEPTH. Darkening, distance pre-delay, room lift and a level dip in one move. The whole plugin lives here.
CRACK. Transient control. Back is tucked, forward is snappy, centre leaves the hit alone.
BODY. 200 Hz shelf, plus or minus 6 dB. Centre is flat.
ROOM. Early reflections and a short 80 to 450 ms tail. First reflection is mono so the snare never drifts.
WIDTH. Widens the room, never the snare.
MIX and OUTPUT. Parallel blend and output trim. BYPASS pill in the header for honest A/B.
PRESETS. Preset bar in the header, save unlimited of your own. Zero latency, so it is safe on the way in or at mixdown.
THE KITE. The little snare on screen is the readout: it hangs from a reel on a kite string and flies exactly as far back as DEPTH says. DRY is the reel, DEEP is the end of the line. DAY or NIGHT in the header picks the sky. It bobs in the breeze, hops on every hit, and the bottom-right corner drags the whole plugin bigger or smaller.
