What Gremlin Duo does

Two of Gremlin's three sections in one plugin. CHOP gates your sound on the tempo grid so a pad, a vocal or a loop turns into a rhythm. SMEAR takes what comes out and melts it: a granular plate that blurs the chops into a wash, pitches it up an octave of shimmer, drifts it, freezes it. Chop feeds Smear, so every stab you cut leaves a tail. Both sections have their own ON pill, so you can run either alone.

Where it comes from

Gremlin is the full mangler: Chop, Stutter and Smear. A lot of people only ever used the first and the last. Duo is those two, same engines, same presets behaviour, for $7. If you want the Stutter rack later, Gremlin is the upgrade.

CHOP, six knobs

RATE. 1/4 to 1/32 with triplets, locked to your DAW tempo.
PATTERN. STRAIGHT, OFFBEAT, 3-3-2, GALLOP, RANDOM.
LENGTH and SHAPE. How much of each step you hear, and whether the edge is a soft pluck or a hard gate.
SWING and HUMANIZE. Up to 66% MPC swing, plus random timing and level drift so it breathes.

SMEAR, ten knobs

SMEAR. How much of the signal goes into the cloud.
PITCH and SHIMMER. Pitch the grains, add the octave-up halo.
BLUR, WASH, DECAY. Grain size, plate amount and how long the tail hangs.
DEPTH, TONE, DRIFT. Modulation depth, tilt of the tail, slow wander of the grain positions.
FREEZE. Pill. Holds the cloud where it is for as long as you like.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Duo on something that sustains. A pad, a chord stab, a vocal, a drum loop.
2. Press play. Both sections open ON. You already hear chopped rhythm with a soft tail.
3. Pick a preset. Chomp & Haze, Half-Time Melt, Gallop Wash, Offbeat Shimmer, Bite & Bloom, Taffy Pull, Jawbreaker Jam, Syrup Stomp and Frosted Teeth, plus Init. Then turn the knobs.

Make it yours

THE MASCOTS. The green chomper bites every time the gate opens. The purple blob glows with the smear. They are the readout.
MIX and OUT. Blend with the dry signal, trim the result. BYPASS pill in the header for an honest A/B.
PRESETS. 10 to start, save unlimited of your own. Right-click or Cmd-click any knob to send it home.
