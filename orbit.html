What Orbit does

Orbit moves sound around the listener in real 3D, not just left-right. Darkening, micro-delay and doppler make the sound physically circle your head. Headphone candy that still works on speakers.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Orbit on a track. Hi-hats, arps, textures, ad-libs: anything you want flying in circles.
2. Press play and watch it go. The orbit visual shows exactly where your sound is right now. What you see is what you hear.
3. Set the RATE. Tempo-synced by default, so the orbit locks to your song. Or switch to Free and set any speed in Hz.

Make it yours

WIDTH. How wide the circle is. Narrow is subtle movement, wide is full flyby.
DEPTH. How much front-to-back distance the orbit has. This is what makes it 3D instead of ping-pong.
TILT. Tips the orbit so the path leans, like a ring around a planet.
SYNC. Lock the orbit to bars and beats, or free-run for drifting ambient motion.

Pro tip

Put Orbit on your hat bus at a slow 4-bar rate with medium width. The groove starts breathing without the listener ever knowing why.
