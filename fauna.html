What Fauna does

Fauna turns animal voices into a real playable instrument. Whale moans, birdsong trills, loon calls: six species you can play with a keyboard, bend with expression, and sink underwater with one knob.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Fauna on a MIDI track. Fauna is an instrument, so add it like any synth.
2. Pick a species. Each tile is a different creature with its own voice. Blue whale for the deep stuff, songbird for the high stuff.
3. Play. Low notes moan, high notes cry. Hold notes and let the creature breathe. Chords work and sound like a flock.

Make it yours

GLIDE and TRILL. Glide slides between notes the way real animals do. Trill adds the flutter that makes birdsong sound alive.
BREATH and BODY. Shape how airy or how full the creature voice is.
OCEAN. One knob that puts everything underwater. Distant, deep, and instantly cinematic.
ATTACK and RELEASE. Slow attack for ambient swells, fast for expressive calls.

Pro tip

Layer a whale two octaves under your pad, drench it in OCEAN, and you have a sub layer with a soul. Producers will ask what sample pack it is. It is not a sample pack.
