Get started in 3 steps

1. Put Ghost on a track. In your DAW, drop Ghost on any track that has sound on it: a vocal, a synth, a whole loop.
2. Hit FREEZE while it plays. Ghost traps the last four seconds of audio. The music keeps going, but now Ghost is holding a frozen copy for you.
3. Move the XY pad. Drag around the pad to travel through the frozen sound. Different spots sound completely different. When you find magic, keep it.

Make it yours

RANDOMIZE and seeds. Every seed number is a different haunting. Type a seed in, get the same character back any time. Found one you love? Write the number down or save it.
Favorites. Save the spots you love and recall them instantly.
Presets. 14 to start, from Seance and Poltergeist to Tape Wraith and Whisper Vault. SAVE keeps your own, every knob included.
Back and forward. Rolled past a good one? The arrows beside RANDOMIZE walk back through everything you heard.
Mix it in. Blend the frozen ghost under the dry sound for texture, or go full wet for pads and transitions.

Pro tip

Freeze a vocal, go full wet, and record long passes to a new track while you ride the XY pad. Chop the best moments: instant custom pad kit.
