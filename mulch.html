What Mulch does

Drag up to 8 samples into the chipper. Any samples. A drum break, a vocal chop, a piano note, a recording of birds outside your window. Mulch chews the whole pile into grains and births ONE new playable instrument out of everything you fed it. Every key you press regrows the sound from random little pieces of your pile, so no two notes are ever exactly the same.

New in v2.0: hold a note, it never dies

Version 1 notes faded out when the grains ran dry. Not anymore. v2.0 has a real ADSR envelope (attack, decay, sustain, release) and regenerating granulation, so a held key keeps growing fresh grains forever. Slow attack plus long release turns any pile into an endless evolving pad. Short and snappy makes it a playable keys patch.

New in v2.0: the RANDOMIZE dice

One click on the dice re-rolls the whole machine into a sound you did not know it had. Every roll is stamped with a SEED number, so when the dice lands on something magic, write the seed down. Type it back in later and you get the exact same instrument back. Reproducible chaos.

PITCH SCATTER. Grains wander away from the note you played. A little is detune shimmer, a lot is a choir of strangers.
GRAIN SIZE JITTER. Every grain gets its own random size. The texture stops being a grid and starts being alive.
REVERSE PROBABILITY. The chance any grain plays backwards. Instant tape-ghost flavor.
FILTER DRIFT. A slow random filter walk per voice, so long notes shift color while they sustain.
PAN SCATTER. Grains land all over the stereo field instead of down the middle.
START-POSITION CHAOS. Grains grab pieces from random spots in your samples, not just where you pointed.

The knobs

MULCH. The big one. At zero, notes grow from your first sample only. Turn it up and the grains start coming from EVERYTHING in the pile at once. Full mulch = total hybrid.
CHEW. Grain size. Tiny confetti pieces for shimmering clouds, big chunks for recognizable slices of your source.
SPIN. Chaos. Random pitch throws up to a fifth in either direction, plus a chance any grain plays backwards. A little is texture, a lot is a haunted blender.
GLUE. Melts the grains into one voice: a warming lowpass plus a soft squash so the pile stops sounding like pieces and starts sounding like an instrument.
ADSR. Full attack, decay, sustain and release. New in v2.0: sustain actually sustains, forever, because the granulator keeps regenerating under your finger.
SMEAR / FREEZE. SMEAR blurs the grains into a soft texture wash. FREEZE locks the grain cloud in place so it hangs like fog while you play over it.
CHAOS. The macro. One knob that leans all six randomization params in at once. Gentle drift at 9 o'clock, haunted blender at full.
OUT. Output trim.

Watch it eat

The chipper on the front panel is not decoration. Your samples sit in the tray as colored chips, and when you play, the gears grind and the chute sprays grain confetti in each chip's color, so you can literally see whose pieces are in the sound right now. Click a chip to take it out of the pile.

What to feed it

Melodic stuff at similar pitches mulches into playable hybrids. Drums plus anything = instant texture percussion. One vocal plus one synth is the classic. Field recordings turn everything into a film score. There is no wrong pile.
